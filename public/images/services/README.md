Drop your own photos here for the "Everything you need to travel Sri
Lanka comfortably" cards on the homepage. Each card looks for one exact
filename below — just save your photo with that name and it appears
automatically, no code changes needed.

  airport-transfers.jpg       -> "Airport Transfers" card
  private-day-tours.jpg       -> "Private Day Tours & Excursions" card
  custom-tour-packages.jpg    -> "Custom Sri Lanka Tour Packages" card
  car-van-hire.jpg            -> "Private Car & Van Hire with Driver" card

Recommended: landscape photos, at least 800x450px (16:9-ish), under
~500KB each so the site stays fast. JPG or PNG both work.

Until a photo is added, that card shows a clean placeholder instead of
a broken image — so it's safe to leave any of these empty for now.
